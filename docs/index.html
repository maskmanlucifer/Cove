Cove

Say it once. Cove remembers the rest.

Cove is a calm, voice-first companion for Android. It works offline, and it keeps your data in an encrypted database on your phone.

  • Alarms, to-dos and a daily plan
  • Habits and a simple workout plan
  • Money, with payments read from bank messages on your phone
  • A journal with text, photos and voice notes
  • A spoken morning brief

Cloud sync, Drive and Gemini are optional. You connect your own accounts.
